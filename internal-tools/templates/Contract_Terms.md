# Freelance Services Agreement

This Freelance Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of [Insert Date], by and between [Client Name], located at [Client Address] (the "Client"), and Carlos Munoz, a Freelance Marketing Analyst, located in California (the "Freelancer"), collectively referred to as the "Parties."

---

## 1. Scope of Work

The Freelancer agrees to provide marketing analytics services as described in the Scope of Work (SOW), attached hereto or outlined in a separate document agreed upon by both parties. These services may include analytics reporting, campaign tracking, dashboard development, data audits, and related deliverables.

## 2. Term & Termination

This Agreement shall commence on the date above and remain in effect until terminated by either party. Either party may terminate this Agreement with 7 days' written notice. Upon termination, the Client agrees to compensate the Freelancer for all completed work up to the termination date.

## 3. Payment Terms

The Client agrees to pay the Freelancer as outlined in the agreed Scope of Work or payment schedule. Payments are due within 10 business days upon receipt of an invoice. Late payments may delay deliverables and are subject to a 2% monthly late fee.

## 4. Independent Contractor Status

The Freelancer is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Client. As such, the Freelancer shall not be entitled to any employee benefits, insurance, or tax withholdings. The Freelancer assumes full responsibility for the payment of all federal, state, and local taxes, including self-employment taxes, pursuant to the Internal Revenue Code and California law.

## 5. California Labor Compliance

This Agreement complies with California's labor laws, including Assembly Bill 5 (AB5). The Freelancer certifies that they operate as an independent business, maintain control over their own work schedule, and provide services to multiple clients. This Agreement does not establish an employer-employee relationship.

In accordance with California Business and Professions Code Section 740, both parties agree that this freelance relationship does not violate state labor protections. Freelancer is responsible for maintaining all necessary licenses and registrations required to operate legally in California.

## 6. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep all proprietary and confidential information, including trade secrets, marketing data, and analytics insights, private and secure. This obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement.

## 7. Intellectual Property

Upon full payment, all work products created by the Freelancer for the Client under this Agreement shall become the sole property of the Client. The Freelancer retains the right to showcase non-sensitive portions of work in their portfolio with written permission.

## 8. Liability

The Freelancer shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from the use of services or deliverables. The Freelancer's liability is limited to the total compensation received under this Agreement.

## 9. Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be resolved through good faith negotiations. If unresolved, the parties agree to mediation in the State of California prior to any legal action. The prevailing party in any legal action shall be entitled to reasonable attorney's fees and costs.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California.

## 11. Entire Agreement

This Agreement represents the entire understanding between the Parties and supersedes all prior negotiations or agreements. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
