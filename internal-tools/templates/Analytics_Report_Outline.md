# Analytics Report Outline

*Use this structure to deliver clear, professional, and client-ready analytics reports.*

---

## 1. Executive Summary

- Quick snapshot of key takeaways
- High-level performance summary
- Main recommendations

## 2. Objectives

- Campaign or business goals
- KPIs (e.g., conversions, CTR, ROI, bounce rate, ROAS)

## 3. Data Sources

- Platforms tracked (Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Shopify, etc.)
- Tracking methods used (UTM parameters, custom events, custom dimensions)

---

## 4. Performance Overview

### Traffic Summary

- Total visits, sessions, unique users
- Top channels: Organic, Paid, Social, Direct, Referral

### Engagement Metrics

- Avg. session duration
- Bounce / engagement rate
- Pages per session

### Conversion Metrics

- Goal completions
- Conversion rate by channel
- Funnel drop-off points

## 6. User Behavior Insights

- Top-performing pages
- Heatmap analysis (if applicable)
- New vs. returning users
- Device & location breakdown

---

## 7. A/B Test Results (if applicable)

- Test summary and hypothesis
- Winning variation
- Impact on KPIs

---

## 8. Recommendations

- What's working and why
- What needs improvement
- Prioritized next steps

---

## 9. Appendix

- Raw data tables (if needed)
- Glossary of terms
- Methodology notes
