## Overview

This repository documents marketing consulting work completed as a **Marketing Advisor** at the [NorCal Small Business Development Center (SBDC)](https://norcalsbdc.org/), part of the [America's SBDC Network](https://americassbdc.org/), funded through a cooperative agreement with the [U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA)](https://www.sba.gov/).

The SBDC provides no-cost, confidential business advising and education to small business owners across Northern California. As an advisor, I managed a portfolio of 15+ active clients simultaneously, delivering data-driven marketing strategy, technical audits, and execution-ready tools across a wide range of industries and business stages.

Client names have been anonymized to protect confidentiality.

## Analytics & Technical Marketing

**Technical SEO**
Conducted technical SEO audits using Screaming Frog, Google Search Console, and Google Rich Results Test — identifying critical on-page deficiencies including 100% duplicate page titles and zero structured data implementation across multiple client websites.

**Paid Media Analysis**
Built a paid media performance framework covering Google Ads and Meta Ads with campaign-level ROAS analysis, CPL trend tracking across a six-month window, and a budget pacing dashboard with projected end-spend calculations.

**Customer Segmentation**
Developed an 8-segment RFM customer segmentation model with behavioral profiles, revenue distribution analysis, and a color-coded segment activation roadmap — delivered as both a strategy document and a multi-tab Excel workbook with live formulas.

**GEO / AI Search Readiness**
Researched and documented Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) strategy for Shopify e-commerce clients, including JSON-LD schema markup recommendations, AI search readiness validation tools, and structured data implementation guidance targeting citation in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

## Digital Marketing Strategy

**Social Media**
Developed social media strategies for six clients across Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Facebook — including platform-specific content pillars, posting schedules backed by engagement research, caption templates, and hashtag frameworks.

**AI-Assisted Content Workflows**
Designed workflows integrating Claude, Canva AI, and Buffer that reduce weekly social media content creation time by up to 80% for solo practitioners. Built a comprehensive walkthrough for a healthcare client including a copy-paste Claude system prompt pre-loaded with the client's brand voice, tone, and local targeting parameters.

**Early Childhood Education Market Research**
Conducted competitive research on the California TK expansion and its impact on private preschool enrollment — identifying 150+ closures in LA County and analyzing Google Ads versus Meta Ads CPL benchmarks for a constrained $10/day marketing budget.
